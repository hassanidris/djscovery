## How It Works

**Section Title**: Simple Application Process

**Step 1: Apply**
> Fill out a short application with your DJ name, contact info, portfolio links, and a brief description of your style and experience.

**Step 2: We Review**
> Our team reviews your application within 2 weeks. We check your portfolio, social presence, and fit with our community.

**Step 3: Get Verified**
> If approved, we'll send you an email verification link to confirm your identity. This keeps our community authentic.

**Step 4: Create Your Profile**
> Once verified, you'll create your full DJ profile on DJcovery. Your founding status is activated immediately.

**Step 5: Start Building**
> Your profile goes live with your founding badge. You get 12 months of premium and priority ranking from our public launch date.

## FAQ

**Question**: How many founding DJs will there be?
**Answer**: We're capping founding membership at 100 DJs to maintain exclusivity and badge value. Once we reach the cap, we'll open a waitlist for future cohorts.

**Question**: What happens after my 12 months of premium end?
**Answer**: After your founding premium year ends, you can choose to continue with a paid premium plan or switch to our free tier. Your founding badge stays with you forever.

**Question**: Can I apply if I don't have a DJ profile on DJcovery yet?
**Answer**: Yes! In fact, most founding DJs will be new to the platform. You'll create your profile as part of the onboarding process after approval.

**Question**: What if I get rejected?
**Answer**: If your application isn't approved, you can reapply after 30 days. We'll provide feedback on why, so you can strengthen your application for next time.

**Question**: Is there a cost to apply or become a founding DJ?
**Answer**: No. Applying is free, and there's no cost to become a founding DJ. The 12 months of premium and priority ranking are our gift to early supporters.

**Question**: What's the difference between a founding DJ and a verified DJ?
**Answer**: The founding badge is permanent and marks you as an early supporter. The verified badge is based on your plan level and can be earned by any DJ through premium membership. You can have both badges.
