# Event-Anchored DJ Reviews - User Guide

## Overview

Event-anchored DJ reviews allow you to review DJs in the context of specific events you've attended, providing more meaningful and trustworthy feedback for the community.

## What are Event-Anchored Reviews?

### Traditional Direct Reviews
- General reviews about a DJ's performance
- Not tied to any specific event
- Can be submitted anytime

### Event-Anchored Reviews
- Reviews tied to a specific event you attended
- More contextual and trustworthy
- Must be submitted within 30 days of the event
- Only available for events you actually attended

## Review Types

### 1. Direct Reviews
- **Who can submit**: Any authenticated user
- **When**: Anytime
- **Context**: General DJ performance
- **Limit**: One direct review per DJ per user

### 2. Event Attendee Reviews
- **Who can submit**: Users who attended the event
- **When**: Within 30 days of event completion
- **Context**: Specific event performance
- **Limit**: One review per DJ per event per user
- **Badge**: Blue "Attendee" badge

### 3. Event Organizer Reviews
- **Who can submit**: Event organizers (auto-detected)
- **When**: Within 30 days of event completion
- **Context**: Professional interaction and performance
- **Limit**: One review per DJ per event per user
- **Badge**: Blue "Organizer" badge

## How to Submit Reviews

### Submitting a Direct Review

1. **Navigate to DJ Profile**
   - Search for the DJ or browse DJ listings
   - Click on the DJ's profile

2. **Click "Write Review"**
   - Find the "Write Review" button in the Crowd Feedback section
   - Click to open the review modal

3. **Fill in Your Review**
   - Select star rating (1-5)
   - Write your review (30-2000 characters)
   - The form will show "Direct review" in the header

4. **Submit**
   - Click "Submit Review"
   - You'll see a success confirmation
   - The modal will close automatically

### Submitting an Event-Anchored Review

1. **Attend an Event**
   - Mark yourself as "Going" or "Interested" in the event
   - Actually attend the event
   - Your attendance will be verified

2. **Wait for Event Completion**
   - Events must be marked as "COMPLETED" before reviews can be submitted
   - This typically happens shortly after the event ends

3. **Visit the Event Page**
   - Go to the event's page
   - Look for the "Review the DJs" section

4. **Select a DJ to Review**
   - You'll see a list of DJs who performed at the event
   - DJs you've already reviewed will show a "Reviewed" badge
   - Click "Review" next to a DJ you haven't reviewed

5. **Fill in Your Review**
   - Select star rating (1-5)
   - Write your review (30-2000 characters)
   - The form will show the event context (event name, date, location)
   - Different placeholder text for organizers vs attendees

6. **Submit**
   - Click "Submit Review"
   - Your review will be tied to that specific event
   - The review will show an event badge linking back to the event

## Review Guidelines

### Content Requirements
- **Minimum length**: 30 characters
- **Maximum length**: 2000 characters
- **Rating**: Must select 1-5 stars
- **Language**: Be respectful and constructive

### What to Include in Your Review
- **For Direct Reviews**: Overall impression, music selection, professionalism, communication
- **For Event Reviews**: Specific event performance, crowd engagement, song choices, stage presence
- **For Organizer Reviews**: Punctuality, professionalism, communication, ease of working together

### What to Avoid
- Personal attacks or offensive language
- Fake or misleading reviews
- Reviews for events you didn't attend
- Multiple reviews for the same DJ/event combination
- Reviews outside the 30-day window for event reviews

## Viewing and Filtering Reviews

### On DJ Profiles
1. **All Reviews Tab**: Shows all review types merged together
2. **Direct Tab**: Only direct reviews (no event context)
3. **Events Tab**: Only event-anchored reviews (attendee + organizer)
4. **Gigs Tab**: Only gig reviews from organizers

### Review Badges
- **Gray "Direct"**: Traditional direct review
- **Blue "Attendee"**: Event attendee review
- **Blue "Organizer"**: Event organizer review
- **Amber "Gig-Organizer"**: Gig review from organizer

### Event Context
- Event-anchored reviews show:
  - Event name (clickable link to event page)
  - Event date
  - Event location
  - Review type badge

## Review Limits and Rules

### Per-User Limits
- **Direct reviews**: One per DJ
- **Event reviews**: One per DJ per event
- **Total**: No limit on total number of reviews

### Time Limits
- **Direct reviews**: No time limit
- **Event reviews**: Must be submitted within 30 days of event completion

### Attendance Verification
- You must have attended the event to submit an event-anchored review
- Attendance is verified through the event attendance system
- You cannot review DJs for events you didn't attend

### Self-Review Prevention
- You cannot review your own DJ profile
- This applies to both direct and event-anchored reviews

## Managing Your Reviews

### Editing Reviews
- Currently, review editing is not supported
- If you need to change a review, contact support
- Future updates may include edit functionality

### Deleting Reviews
- Contact support to request review deletion
- Provide your username, DJ name, and review details
- Reviews may be removed if they violate community guidelines

### Viewing Your Reviews
- Visit your profile page
- Look for the "My Reviews" section
- You can see all reviews you've submitted

## Troubleshooting

### "You must have attended this event to review"
- **Cause**: You haven't marked attendance for this event
- **Solution**: Make sure you've marked yourself as attending the event before it ended

### "Review window expired (30 days)"
- **Cause**: More than 30 days have passed since the event
- **Solution**: Unfortunately, event reviews cannot be submitted after the 30-day window

### "DJ did not perform at this event"
- **Cause**: The DJ wasn't listed as a performer for this event
- **Solution**: Only review DJs who actually performed at the event

### "Event must be completed before reviewing"
- **Cause**: The event hasn't been marked as completed yet
- **Solution**: Wait for the event to be marked as completed by the organizer

### "You cannot review your own DJ profile"
- **Cause**: You're trying to review your own profile
- **Solution**: You cannot review your own DJ profile

## FAQ

### Q: Can I submit both a direct review and an event review for the same DJ?
**A**: Yes! You can have one direct review and one event review per DJ. They serve different purposes and provide different context.

### Q: What happens if I submit a review for the wrong event?
**A**: Contact support immediately with the details. They can help correct or remove the review.

### Q: Can I change my review type after submitting?
**A**: No, review type is determined at submission time based on whether you include event context.

### Q: Do event reviews count toward the DJ's overall rating?
**A**: Yes, all review types contribute to the DJ's overall rating and reputation.

### Q: Can organizers see who reviewed them?
**A**: Organizers can see review content and usernames, but this is subject to privacy policies.

### Q: What if a DJ performed at multiple events I attended?
**A**: You can submit one review per event. Each review will be tied to its specific event context.

### Q: Are event reviews weighted differently than direct reviews?
**A**: Currently, all reviews contribute equally to the DJ's rating. Future updates may introduce weighting.

### Q: Can I submit a review for a private event?
**A**: Yes, if you attended the private event and it's marked as completed, you can submit a review.
